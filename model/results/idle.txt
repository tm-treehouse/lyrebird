B.  HOW OFTEN DOES IT TRIP ON NEAR-IDLE MATERIAL?

Every figure behind the 1-in-36 came from a 1 kHz tone at -3.7 dBFS.
This is material.py's idle catalogue instead, six rates, six
preambles, so the state the loop is in when the signal goes quiet
differs from trial to trial. The preamble is discarded; 2**19
element samples of it sit between the last of it and the first
measured sample, which is longer than the cascade's settling, so the
ring on the transition is not in the window. Getting that wrong
reads as -50 dBFS of low-frequency energy and looks exactly like the
fault.
