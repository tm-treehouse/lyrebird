==========================================================================
THE INSTRUMENT, CHECKED AGAINST THE TONE MEASUREMENT   [0 s]
==========================================================================
Signal-to-noise against a tone excises the signal lobe. Broadband
material has no lobe to excise, so what is measured instead is the
error against the ideal reconstruction of the same PCM, with a
scalar gain fitted in band and removed -- the broadband equivalent
of excising the fundamental. Before trusting it on material, it is
run on the one signal where the old instrument also works.

Same record, same chain, two instruments. 1 kHz at -3.7 dBFS, one
percent elements, the datapath at its settled widths.

The error instrument reads +1.24 dB against classic SNDR on average, spread +1.16 to +1.37 dB.
The offset is expected and its two causes are known. The classic
figure rescales the surviving noise bins to cover the ones it
excised -- ten harmonic lobes and the signal lobe out of about 850
in-band bins, which is +1.1 dB of noise it did not measure. The error
figure counts harmonics as error, which pushes the other way. Every
material number below is on the error instrument, so the comparison
against 132.8 dB carries this offset and it is stated rather than
hidden.

The alignment margin is how much worse the in-band error gets if
the two paths are slid 256 samples apart. It is the check that
matters most: the STF of this topology is exactly 1 with zero delay,
so a wrong settling offset would show up here rather than as a
confidently wrong noise figure. A plain lag argmax cannot do this
job -- see the traps section at the end.
